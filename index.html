Efficacy & Performance Trials

End-to-end trials measuring how effectively biological and chemical crop protection products control target pests, weeds, and diseases.

These trials also include adjuvant evaluations to improve product uptake, delivery, and overall performance.

Crop Safety & Selectivity Trials

Assessing product safety on different crops to ensure no adverse phytotoxic effects, while confirming crop tolerance under various growing conditions.

Biostimulant & Plant Health Trials

Evaluating biostimulants in enhancing plant resilience, improving stress tolerance, stimulating growth, and supporting overall plant health.

Plant Growth Regulator (PGR) Trials

Testing plant growth regulators to optimize key developmental stages, manage vegetative and reproductive growth, influence flowering and fruiting, and improve crop uniformity and yield potential.

Fertilizer Trials

Nutrition trials to assess how fertilizer treatments support plant health, stimulate growth, and increase yield for optimal crop performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

CropIntelek conducts a wide range of agricultural research trials including efficacy testing, crop selectivity studies, biostimulant evaluations, plant growth assessments, fertilizer and adjuvant trials. We specialize in field trials, glasshouse studies, tunnel trials, and aerial applications across major bio-climatic zones in South Africa.

We work across multiple crop categories including fruit crops, row crops, grains, vegetables, and pasture or grass species. Our expertise covers key South African crops such as maize, soybeans, wheat, citrus, table and wine grapes, stone fruit, potatoes, and various vegetables. Trial protocols are tailored to each crop and its specific growing region to ensure accurate and meaningful results.

All research is conducted in alignment with EPPO (European and Mediterranean Plant Protection Organization) guidelines and reported using ARM (Agricultural Research Manager) software, following GEP (Good Experimental Practice) principles. We also maintain compliance with South African regulatory requirements under the Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development (DALRRD).

Trial duration varies depending on crop growth cycles and research objectives. We provide a project-specific timeline during planning, and maintain ongoing progress communication throughout the season to ensure on-time reporting and delivery.
