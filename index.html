About the Company

Who we are

CropIntelek delivers high‑quality agricultural research solutions. Our team of field investigators brings up to 20 years' experience running trials in all major bio‑climatic zones of South Africa. We focus on thorough methodologies and clear, high‑quality reports.

Our comprehensive approach to agricultural research involves everything from initial protocol development to final data analysis and regulatory reporting. We specialize in conducting precise field trials that provide actionable insights for crop protection products, biostimulants, and agricultural inputs across diverse South African growing conditions.

With extensive experience in both commercial and registration trials, CropIntelek ensures that all studies meet international standards including EPPO (European and Mediterranean Plant Protection Organization) protocols and ARM (Agricultural Research Manual) guidelines. Our expertise spans multiple crop categories including grains, fruits, vegetables, and specialty crops, allowing us to deliver comprehensive research solutions tailored to South Africa's unique agricultural landscape.

We follow best practices established by leading agricultural research organizations including the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) and maintain compliance with South African regulatory requirements through the Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development.

What we do

Registration & Commercial Trials

Request Proposal

We conduct comprehensive agricultural research trials across South Africa, providing reliable data for product registration and commercial development. Our services cover the full spectrum of agricultural research needs, from efficacy testing to crop selectivity studies, ensuring your products meet regulatory requirements and perform optimally in real-world conditions.

Efficacy

End-to-end trials measuring the effectiveness of both biological and chemical crop protection products.

Crop Selectivity

Testing how products perform across different crops to ensure safety and effectiveness.

Bio-Stimulus

Assessing products that enhance plant health, improve stress resilience, and stimulate growth.

Plant Growth Regulator

Testing regulators to optimize development stages, control flowering & fruiting, and improve crop uniformity & productivity.

Fertilizer

Conducting trials to determine fertilizer efficiency, impact on soil health, and overall crop performance.

Adjuvants

Enhancing product delivery, improving formulation compatibility, and boosting effectiveness.
